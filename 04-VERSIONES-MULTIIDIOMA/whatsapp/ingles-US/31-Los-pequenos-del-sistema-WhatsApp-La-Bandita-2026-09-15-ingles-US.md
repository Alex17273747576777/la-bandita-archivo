## ⚡ The little ones, on one page

There are apps that do everything and apps that do ONE thing. These are the second kind: turn off the screen, force the saving, change the hertz, freeze the background. Old-iron philosophy: one single thing, done well. Fourth batch of the Android door — and a batch of debts: here are Piece 30's **six pending names**, now with a verified house… except the ones that honestly don't deserve it yet (below, their list). And an archive joy: **EnforceDoze** — Piece 29's radar — closed its case by the usual method: the link closes the case. **Eleven listings** verified TODAY, September 15.

## 🖥️ The screen

- **Adaptive-Hz** — ★77 · v2.4.0 Beta (Aug 20) · push Sep 14 · https://github.com/mahmutaunal/Adaptive-Hz — changes the screen's hertz depending on what you're doing: fluidity when it matters, battery when it doesn't. In declared beta and moving code yesterday itself.
- **Grayscaler** — ★162 · 1.0 (Feb 2025) · https://github.com/C10udburst/Grayscaler — "Keep your phone mostly monochrome, but allow apps like camera": life in black and white, with exceptions. Still release — the date gets noted.
- **Fold_Switcher** — ★101 · V1.2.2 (Apr 2025) · https://github.com/eiyooooo/Fold_Switcher — for the foldables: switching between folded/unfolded states at will. Small niche, exact trade.
- **SmartIsland** — ★172 · v6.0.0 (Aug 29) · https://github.com/agupta07505/SmartIsland — the free dynamic island: notifications, music and calls as a floating pill, "zero cloud tracking," its house declares.

The link closes the case, the day's fourth time.

## ❓ Little-ones questions

**So many apps for one single thing isn't excess?**
It's the opposite: each one can be removed without the phone mourning it. The excess is the app that does everything halfway and can't release any piece.

**Why do you list such small apps (★56)?**
Because the star decorates and the date rules — and because a utility that delivers can go years without needing more. The little ones don't compete for the summit: they hold up the mountain's foot.
