## ❓ The stores' question

**Is a store a forge?**
No. The forge keeps the code; the store keeps the packaged builds. The house lists them separately — because whoever audits code isn't reading the same thing as whoever installs an APK. Confusing them is like confusing the kitchen with the dining room.
