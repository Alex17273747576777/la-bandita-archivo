## ⚡ The workshop, on one page

The "Android, system, Root and Shizuku" door was the wall's second fullest (197 listings). Its 18 verified got opened TODAY, September 15, and they tell one single story with different trades: **doing with the phone what the factory doesn't allow** — without breaking it, and knowing which permission each key asks for. Eighteen alive, zero archived: the workshop is mid-shift. The house's rule, remembered: the tool gets named here; whatever each person does with root or ADB, they do at their own risk and reading its house.

Tiny in stars, ahead in version number: it comes in with its factory notice — it's third-party development on the mother, not the official one.

The link closes the case: it exists — "A fork of AxManager aimed at minimalism," a declared fork of the mother — and it pushed code yesterday. It isn't dead: it breathes slowly.
