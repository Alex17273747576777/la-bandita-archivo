## ⚡ On one page

There are apps that didn't pick one trade: they put it all together. Manga, anime, light novels — sometimes comics, webtoons, audiobooks — in a single library. This guide opens the complete panorama: the big catalog got seen TODAY in Miyomi (the Multi category, thirty-some apps listed), and here it is filtered with the house's stick — what already has its own guide, what new name enters the radar, and what's still alive at this hour of the day.

## 🏷️ What "multi" means

In the catalogs, each app carries tags for what it joins: **A**nime · **M**anga · **LN** (light novel) · **C**omics · **W**ebtoon · **E**books. Multi is whoever carries two or more. The advantage is obvious: one library, one history, one single learning curve. The price too: more dependencies, more surfaces where something breaks. That's why the house looks at them with a double magnifying glass — and why separate guides exist for each branch.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is multi better than one app per trade?**
It depends on your load: if you read manga and watch anime daily, one hand saves jumps. If you only do one thing, the specialist almost always wins on details.

**Why do some names go without a link?**
Because their house hasn't been opened with this trade yet. The name with a source comes in; the link gets opened the day it's used. That way no address we haven't set foot on gets propagated.

**And is Miyomi an app?**
It's a catalog — this ecosystem's map of maps. Today it let slip its complete Multi category; when the catalog doesn't render, it gets noted that it doesn't render: not one datum gets faked.
