## ❓ Library questions

**Which reader for a cheap eReader?**
The one installed on it: KOReader lives inside the e-ink, not beside it. For the phone, Librera; for organizing from the PC, Calibre — each on its shelf.

**How do I get free books without shady catalogs?**
Through the honest bridge: Project Gutenberg (with Myne as client) has spent fifty-plus years digitizing what the public domain frees. Free, legal and with no suspicious mirrors — the old catalog remains the clean one.
