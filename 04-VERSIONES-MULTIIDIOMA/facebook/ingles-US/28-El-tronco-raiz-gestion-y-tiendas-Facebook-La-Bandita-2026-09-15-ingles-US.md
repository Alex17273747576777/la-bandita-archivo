## ❓ Trunk questions

**Do I need root for all this?**
No: Shizuku, Dhizuku, Hail, the installers and all the stores live root-free. Root (Magisk, KernelSU, APatch) is another faculty — more power, more responsibility, and a warranty you sign yourself.

**Why so many stores if F-Droid exists?**
Because each answers a different need: Obtainium goes to the source, Aurora enters the Play without an account, Accrescent signs everything, Appteka keeps what nobody keeps. One store doesn't compete: it completes.
