## ⚡ The shelf, on one page

The wall's reading door kept 181 listings. The verified ones count a hand and a half — and opening them today turned up three surprises: several are **Komikku's little branches** (the family that already has its guide in Piece 16), one is **a 28-thousand-star giant** the wall had hidden among unverified names, and another **lives on your server**, not on your phone. Seven alive, verified source by source TODAY, September 15 — and one that said goodbye toward the Register.

## 🌳 Komikku's little branches

Piece 16 told the story of TachiyomiSY's organized adopted child. Well then: the wall kept three projects born of that same bush — and today we open their houses:

- **Kisara** — ★1 · v1.21.20 (Aug 13) · https://github.com/ifritraen/kisara — "A fork of Komikku with your desired features," it declares of itself. The tree's personal twig: small, alive since August.
- **Tegaki** — ★2 · v1.14.6 (Jul 29) · https://github.com/MyNameHand/Tegaki — "a personal fork of Komikku with a priority-based scanlator filter and a migration hide-behind…". Its feature is trade-grade: it lets you prioritize which translation groups show first and hide what migrated. Two stars, one idea the big family doesn't ship factory-installed.
- **Komikku Sync** — ★0 · no releases · push Aug 26 · https://github.com/dinkelspiel/komikku-sync — "a fork adding state sync between devices," its house says. No published versions yet: it comes in as radar with an open source — it gets an eye kept on it, not a prescription.

*Family note:* Komikku itself (komikku-app, ★4,712, v1.14.1) lives in Piece 16 — here only its twigs.

## 🦉 The giant the wall was hiding

**Koodo Reader** — ★**28,199** · v2.4.4 (Sep 2) · push of TODAY itself · https://github.com/koodo-reader/koodo-reader

"A modern ebook manager and reader with sync and backup capacities for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android," it declares of itself. The wall had it on its reading door as "Watched / active" — and only now does its size show: it's one of the whole compendium's biggest houses. Multi-library, sync and backup across the five systems. If your EPUB library outgrew your phone, this is the wide shelf.

## 📡 The reader living on your server

**Codexa** — ★64 · v2.6.2 (Sep 11) · push Sep 14 · https://github.com/thehijacker/codexa

"A self-hosted EPUB web reader with multi-user support, OPDS browsing, KOReader sync, and a built-in…", its house declares. It's Piece 17's natural neighbor (Komga and Kavita): another that lives on your server and serves books to the whole house — with KOReader progress sync included. The wall's shelf and the server's bookcase, the same conversation.

## 🀄 The manga that translates itself

**Nyora** — ★34 · 2.7.3 (Jul 31) · push Aug 10 · https://github.com/Nyora-Manga/nyora-android

"Free, ad-free, open-source manga/manhwa/manhua reader with AI page translation," it declares of itself. And here the shelf crosses Piece 02: Kototoro brings OCR translation on the phone; Nyora puts it in a pure manga reader. The same idea, two kitchens — both open source, both verified.

**Readwide** — ★28 · v1.0.18 (Sep 11) · https://github.com/k1717/Readwide — "File reader / manager": the shelf's Swiss knife. It reads and manages files; no bigger pretensions, with last week's release.

## ❓ Two shelf questions

**Why so many Komikku twigs together?**
Because the wall kept them scattered among 181 listings. Counted in their context, they tell a story: Piece 16's family has small branches with features of their own — scanlator filters, cross-device sync — worth more together than lost.

**Do the small listings (★1, ★2, ★0) deserve a guide?**
The star isn't the stick — the opened source and the date are. Kisara has an August release; Komikku Sync doesn't publish versions yet and goes as radar. Each with its own state, dated.
