## ❓ Last-door questions

**And now what, if the catalog ran out?**
The catalog was a candidate, not a destination. Now the rest begins: the house's general classifier — everything listed, tagged by rank and class — and the doors that come from no catalog at all. The map doesn't end: it changes basin.

**Why celebrate a closing knowing it will reopen?**
Because perfect would be a dead end: the house prefers honest cutoffs and re-entries. Tomorrow somebody launches something and the catalog has doors again. That's how one works better than what preceded — without pretending to be perfect.
