## ⚡ On one page

There was an archive with everything: a thousand-some listings of free tools, piled layer over layer, update over update, until it became a wall impossible to read or to publish. Today its rescue operation begins: **subtract, organize, divide, update and correct**. The result, announced and started TODAY: a library of **75 publications** (within the 50–80 range), each in Facebook and WhatsApp format, counting the 20 guides this house already has published. This is the building's plan.

## 🧱 What was there and why it didn't work

**The V25** — "La Bandita's Open Sauce," update 016 — was a 2.4-megabyte document: 1,029 listings piled up in 16 layers, with the same sections repeated over and over, and data dated September 2. Thirteen days later, part of it already expired (Art. 63: dated history). A wall like that doesn't get published: it gets exhumed.

## 🔢 What the count says

The 1,029 listings counted, one by one:

- **~82 verified** to some degree — repositories confirmed and projects observed in their day. First-quality material.
- **940 "registered identity"** — a name and a source, nothing more. They aren't listings: they're **candidates**. They'll come in when their source gets opened and verifies the day they're used. Not one address gets propagated without setting foot in its house.
- 1 archived, 6 with no state — to the Register, not to the map.

## 📐 The plan: 75 publications

- **00–19 — the existing ones:** the 20 guides of the September 14 cutoff. Not rewritten except for incoherence.
- **20 — this piece:** the project's index.
- **21–29 — Music:** players, streaming, podcasts, lyrics, radio, tools. **30–37 — Android/system:** Shizuku, root, backup, files, launchers, firewall. **38–45 — Reading:** EPUB, PDF, comics, community clients, RSS. **46–52 — Anime and video:** players, media center, all-in-one. **53–57 — Libraries:** metadata and collections. **58–61 — Self-hosted. 62–63 — Productivity. 64 — Privacy. 65 — Extensions. 66 — Development.**
- **67–74 — reserve:** the new cutoff's LA LISTA and whatever the road brings.

Each guide with the house's format: map, verified listings, care, questions and a meme that doesn't smother.

## 🧪 The filtering stick

- A wall listing enters a guide **only the day its primary source gets opened and verifies again**. What was true on September 2 is a hypothesis today.
- The unverified gets named as radar, without a link. The dead goes to the Register. The forbidden (index recipes, keys, mods' APKs, clones) never gets pasted.
- **One batch at a time:** one door per turn, well done, with its verification. Haste isn't method.
- The signature is human or there's no posting.

## ❓ FAQ

**Do the old guides get updated with this?**
Only if there's incoherence. The wall's stuff goes into new guides, not into inflating the existing ones.

**Why 75 and not 80 in one go?**
Because the exact number is decided by verified content, not by desire. 75 is the range's center and leaves margin for whatever shows up alive.

**When will it be complete?**
By batches, one door per turn. The library isn't in a hurry: it has a stick.
