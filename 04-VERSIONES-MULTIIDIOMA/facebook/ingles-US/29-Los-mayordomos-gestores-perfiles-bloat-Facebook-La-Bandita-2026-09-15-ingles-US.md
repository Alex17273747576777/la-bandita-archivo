## ❓ Butler questions

**Is uninstalling factory bloat safe?**
With the right tools (Canta, UAD-NG, via Shizuku or ADB), yes — and what got uninstalled can get reinstalled. What isn't safe is blind deleting: the serious butlers let you read before crossing out. Fly low: look at the package, then decide.

**Which one do I start with if I don't have root?**
All of this guide's live root-free: Shizuku up top (Piece 28), and the butlers below. The work profile (Island/Shelter) doesn't even need a cable.
