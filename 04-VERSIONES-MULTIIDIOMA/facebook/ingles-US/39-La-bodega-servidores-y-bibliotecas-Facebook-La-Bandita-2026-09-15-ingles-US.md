## ❓ Cellar questions

**Do I need a server to read manga on the phone?**
No: the pocket apps are enough. The server is for whoever wants their library on every screen — the mobile only asks, the cellar serves. Everything at its height.

**Which of the three libraries?**
The one for your bookcase: Kavita for a bit of everything, Komga for comic collections in military order, Suwayomi for what your Mihon already has. The use test rules — all three pushed code today, none has a life advantage.
