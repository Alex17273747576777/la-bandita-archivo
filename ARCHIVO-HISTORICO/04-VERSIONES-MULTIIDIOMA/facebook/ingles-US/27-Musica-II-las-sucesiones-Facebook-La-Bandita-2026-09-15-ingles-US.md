## ⚡ The succession, on one page

Free music is the kingdom of inheritances: a project falls asleep, and its forks — its code children — fight for the throne. Piece 08 told the living ones' map; 21 brought the wall's harvest. This guide tells the story neither had told yet: **who gets what**. Verified TODAY, September 15, source by source — and with two discoveries neither the wall nor we had.

## 📜 The ancestor's testament

**InnerTune** — the ancestor (★6,086, push Nov 2025: asleep for ten months, as piece 08 has it). Its testament, counted in forks TODAY: over a hundred code children, and most dead or mirrors — ★0 repos with no versions that only copy the name. Its network's big names (Malopieds, BobbyESP) haven't beaten since 2024–2025. A sleeping king, with a kingdom of ghosts.

## ⭐ The heir that changed its name

**Estrella-Music** — ★45 · v2.3.9 (Aug 22) · push Sep 4 · https://github.com/josprox/Estrella-Music

A **declared fork of InnerTune** (GitHub certifies it the ancestor's child) — and the only living branch with a name of its own, a recent version and code pushed this month. It inherited the ancestor's Material 3 interface ("A Material 3 YouTube Music client for Android," it declares of itself) and gave it a new name. The ancestor's succession has a recipient: still small (45 stars), but breathing — and the family's only one with all three life signals: release, push and own house.

*A method note:* other recently pushed forks (omnizs38, Sep-13 push) turned out to be ★0 mirrors with no versions — they get noted as radar, not as heirs. The date alone succeeds nobody.

## 👑 The house favorite's dynasty

**Metrolist** — the one Piece 08 crowns "the most active" — turned out to have a dynasty of its own, and TODAY itself three generations became clear:

1. **The move to the palace:** Metrolist no longer lives alone under its creator — its house is now the **MetrolistGroup** organization (verified TODAY via its releases channel: same Nightly of Sep 14, same 13.7.0). Old links redirect; the kingdom has big-house structure.
2. **The prodigal daughter:** **Meld** — the harvest's one (piece 21, ★1,584, this morning's push) — **is a fork of Metrolist**. GitHub certifies it: the one that looked like a cousin is a daughter. Its genealogy stands corrected with a date.
3. **The new grandchildren, with today's pulse:**
   - **MetroFuse** — ★167 · v7.2 (Aug 30) · **push TODAY itself** · https://github.com/956tris/MetroFuse — "YouTube Music client and Local player… with the twist of Many different backends and frontends," its house declares: the one wanting to be several players at once.
   - **Iride** — Alpha 27 (Sep 11, two alphas the same day) · https://github.com/Fhox006/Iride — a Metrolist fork with a fast testing cadence. It goes in declared alpha: tested small, everything noted. (Its stars remain to be counted when the query window renews — the listing comes in with what's verified: versions and dates.)
   - And in the historical corner, BobbyESP/Metrolist: the first succession attempt (2025) that fell by the wayside — it gets noted, not prescribed.

## 🌫️ The cutoff's pendings

## ❓ Succession questions

**Which successor do I move to if my favorite app sleeps?**
To the one with all three signals: a dated release, a recent push and an own house. Stars decorate; dates inherit.

**Can a fork with fewer stars be better than its mother?**
It already happened twice in this very guide: Estrella-Music (45) against a sleeping ancestor, and Meld (1,584) moving more code today than its own mother. The throne isn't inherited by the number: it's won by movement.
