## ❓ Current questions

**Do I need to touch this?**
No: the apps ship their default river. This map is for whoever wants to know where they drink from — or open an own channel. Whoever knows where the water comes from knows what to expect.

**Why do so many extensions do the same thing?**
Because each app dug its own — and because the water belongs to everybody: the same source site shows up reinterpreted in family, in French, in Portuguese. It isn't redundancy: it's basin.
