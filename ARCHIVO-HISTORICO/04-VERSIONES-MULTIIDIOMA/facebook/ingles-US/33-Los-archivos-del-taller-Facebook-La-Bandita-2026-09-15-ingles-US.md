## ❓ Files questions

**Another file manager? Doesn't the phone ship one?**
It ships something that opens folders. The ones here bring optional root, no tracking and design — the difference between a drawer and a drawer with labels.

**What's watching a room with an old phone for?**
For knowing if somebody enters where they shouldn't: Haven turns a forgotten phone into an alarm with witnesses. Well-understood paranoia is method.
