The wall had 231 listings on its music door. This guide starts bringing them into the sun: the **12 that carried verification**, opened one by one **TODAY, September 15**. The result: 12 of 12 alive — and two further along than the wall itself (Kopuz and Levyra shipped a version since the wall stored them). This is the Library project's first harvest: from the disastrous compendium, a true map.

## 🧹 Why this guide smells clean

The wall gave each project a literary "mini description" — the same phrase repeated by categories, a hundred times over. That isn't information: it's filler. Not one goes in here: each listing below carries only what **its own repository declares today** — its textual description, its version, its pulse. Where the repo declares nothing, the listing invents nothing.
