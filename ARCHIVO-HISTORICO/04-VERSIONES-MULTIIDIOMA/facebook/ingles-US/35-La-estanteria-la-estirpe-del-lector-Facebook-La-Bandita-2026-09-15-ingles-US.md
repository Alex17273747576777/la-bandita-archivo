## ⚡ The bookcase, on one page

The Android door closed, the Map orders the next one: **Reading** — the bookcase, with its own founding history. This door doesn't start in an app: it starts in a disappearance. And today's listings — thirteen verified by stepping on houses — are all, one way or another, children of the same winter. The first door where genealogy isn't ornament: it's the furniture's index.

## 🪦 The ancestor without a tomb

**Tachiyomi** — the reader that taught Android to read free manga — was felled by Kakao's DMCA in January 2024. Its original page isn't archived: **it doesn't exist** — GitHub returns the void. And today itself, this house's audit confirmed it twice: the ancestor's repository gives 404, and **TachiyomiJ2K**'s — its most famous fork — does too. The lineage survived; the natal house, no. That's why this bookcase gets indexed in genealogies: when the cradle disappears, the last name is all that's left.
