**La Bandita · September 15, 2026 · US English edition (adapted from the Spanish original, piece 72)**

## ⚡ On one page

A complete census isn't only the list of the living: it's the **minute of the ones that don't pass the stick** — with a reason, a date, and no cruelty. These are the minutes of the V25 census at the ceiling's close: legal restrictions, dead doors, forges that block the probe, and impostors that don't even get linked. The house erases nobody from the map: **it notes them where they belong**.

## ❓ The minutes' question

**Why lose pieces to minutes?**
Because a map without minutes lies. The census's value isn't in recommending everything: it's in **everything telling the truth of its state**. The minute is the part of the map where the map admits what it doesn't know — and that makes it a map for real.
