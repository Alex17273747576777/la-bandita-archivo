## ⚡ The study, on one page

The pocket reads novels; **the study collects**. This is Reading's third door — the computer's: big screens, a keyboard, whole libraries on disk. Twelve listings from the Miyomi catalog, all twelve stepped on TODAY, September 15, with the official server's stick and releases' atoms. The day's datum: **the interpreter's house shipped a version this very morning** (koharu 0.83.1, hours after the catalog noted the 0.41). Two certified moves, one succession by fork inheritance, and one house divided into rooms — the living map, as always, ahead of the sleeping map.

## 🖥️ The big table's readers

- **anx-reader** — ★8,853 · v1.15.0-alpha.25 (Sep 12) · https://github.com/Anxcye/anx-reader — the shelf's most followed: a Flutter book reader with AI capabilities, for Windows, macOS and Linux. A healthy alpha cadence: code gets pushed every few days.
- **Thorium Reader** — ★2,870 · push Sep 14 · https://github.com/edrlab/thorium-reader — EDRLab's EPUB reader over Readium: the open standard in a desktop suit. A million downloads in the catalog and its house pushing code this week. Big publications get read here with dignity.
- **OpenComic** — ★1,969 · v1.6.5 (Oct 31) · push TODAY · https://github.com/ollm/OpenComic — comic and manga in Electron: manga mode, webtoon, double page, floating magnifier. The nuance of a living house with a still release (eleven months): like Miru on the big door — the repo pushes TODAY, the stable version waits. With official stores (Microsoft, Mac, Snap, Flathub).
- **YACReader** — ★1,373 · 10.3.0 (Aug 31) · https://github.com/YACReader/yacreader — the desktop comic classic (Qt, no extra electricity): **1.73 million downloads — this whole shelf's most downloaded.** Twenty years of trade, an August version.
- **Yomikiru** — ★552 · v2.25.0-beta.7 (Sep 7) · https://github.com/mienaiyami/yomikiru — offline reading of manga/webtoon/EPUB in Electron+React: zen mode, portable USB, and a family detail: **compatible with Tachidesk's download structure** — what the cellar's server brings down, the study reads. Syncs with AniList.
- **Free Manga Downloader 2** — ★1,138 · 2.0.34.5 (Mar 17) · push TODAY · https://github.com/dazedcat19/FMD2 — the manga downloader for Windows, a certified heir of the original FMD (a signed fork of the mother project). Still release, house pushing code today itself.

## 🤖 The interpreter

- **koharu** — ★5,561 · **0.83.1 — SHIPPED TODAY ITSELF** · https://github.com/koharu-rs/koharu — the shelf's rare jewel: **it doesn't read, it translates.** An ML manga translator written in Rust (Tauri): bubble detection, OCR, generative inpainting to erase the original text, and a local LLM for the translation. The catalog carried 0.41 of Sep 11 — four days later it's already at 0.83: it's the fastest-running project in the whole study. Certified move: born in mayocream, it lives in koharu-rs.

## 📱 The pocket's guests

They came looking for a library and stayed in the study — with their honest platform label:

- **IReader** — ★938 · v2.0.26-patch2 (Sep 14) · https://github.com/IReaderorg/IReader — a novel reader **for Android AND desktop** (Windows/macOS/Linux: AI TTS, keyboard shortcuts, multi-window). A double listing in one. And the houses talk: it consumes LNReader's sources — and it pushed ten versions in one week against what the catalog said.
- **LNReader** — ★2,834 · v2.1.3 (Aug 22) · push TODAY · https://github.com/lnreader/lnreader — light novels on Android; **certified move: the LNReader org is now lnreader** (new lowercase, same house).
- **QuickNovel** — ★1,820 · "Storage Fixes" (Aug 25) · https://github.com/LagradOst/QuickNovel — downloading novels to read without network. Android.
- **Openlib** — ★2,475 · v1.0.11 (Jan 18) · https://github.com/dstark5/Openlib — searches, downloads and reads from Anna's Archive with a built-in viewer (EPUB/PDF). Flutter, Android. Still since January — healthy, but in no hurry.
- **Paperback** — ★782 · 0.8.11 r2 · https://github.com/Paperback-iOS/app — the comic reader of **iOS/App Store** (also macOS on Apple Silicon). A divided house: the public repo keeps releases, issues and extensions; **it integrates with the cellar's Komga** via extensions — the ecosystem (inkdex, TheNetsky and company) is material for the extensions piece. 146 thousand downloads in the catalog.

## 🧬 The day's moves and successions

Three patterns signed by git itself: **LNReader → lnreader** (org renamed), **mayocream/koharu → koharu-rs/koharu** (a project growing into an organization), **FMD → FMD2** (succession by certified fork — the child continues the father's work). And one leaf to the nursery: `Ayowel/fork-yomikiru`, a Yomikiru fork with no releases — watched, not recommended.

## ❓ Study questions

**Why read on the PC if I have my phone?**
Long collections, huge PDFs, EPUBs with notes, double-page comics: the big screen doesn't compete with the pocket — it completes it. And the cellar's servers turn the desktop into the whole house's reading room.

**And where do the sources come from?**
As always in this house: each reader with its geography — LNReader feeds IReader, FMD2 downloads for Yomikiru and company, Paperback drinks from Komga. The extensions piece (on its way) will map that river.
