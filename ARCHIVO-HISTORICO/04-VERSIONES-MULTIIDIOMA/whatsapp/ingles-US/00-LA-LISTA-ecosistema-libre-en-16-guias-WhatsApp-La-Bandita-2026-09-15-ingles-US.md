## 🗂️ The catalogs (the map of maps)

The directories La Bandita went through to build and feed this list — used as a starting point, never as verification:

- **Miyomi** (miyomi.app) — the Tachiyomi/Mihon ecosystem's directory: apps, warehouses and guides. Its catalog lives in the browser session (no public JSON): it gets read in the browser, unhurried.
- **The Index** (theindex.moe) — collections curated by hand by its editorial team: from no-log VPNs (the court-proven distinction included) to manga and anime apps for Android. The 18+ sections get named with no index.
- **EverythingMoe** (everythingmoe.com) — a curated toplist of otaku culture (anime, manga, manhwa, apps, music), refreshed every few hours, with its own graveyard and an outage detector.
- **Wotaku** (wotaku.wiki) — a wiki by sections: webs, software per system, extensions, guides and glossary.
- **FMHY** (fmhy.net) — the free-software leads wiki (piece 12 of this collection): discovery yes, verification no.

The rule with all of them: the catalog opens the door; the project's house closes the case. The catalogs indexing streaming sites and aggregators get read as a terrain map — nothing gets copied or prescribed here.
