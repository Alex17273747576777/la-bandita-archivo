## ⚡ The face, on one page

The trunk holds up (Piece 28), the butlers administer (29) — and the face is the first thing your phone shows the world: the launcher, the wallpaper, the keyboard, the colors. But a pretty face is only worth it if what's below holds it up — that's why this batch got verified like the previous ones: stepping on houses, reading dates, flying low. **Eleven listings** verified TODAY, September 15, with two move certificates and one complete lineage. Third batch of the Android door.

## ❓ Face questions

**Does changing launcher or keyboard break anything?**
No: they're layers that get tried and removed. The phone returns to its factory face when you remove them. The irreversible is another thing — and that's why it lives in other guides.

**How do I know if a launcher is alive?**
The same as always: the date, not the ornament. Lawnchair compiled a Nightly today; Neo-Launcher pushed today with a 2022 release. Two different ways of being alive — and only one of being dead.
