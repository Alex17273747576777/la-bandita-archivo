## ⚡ On one page

There's an app that searches your book across the three big libraries at once, downloads it the fast way, and leaves you reading it on the same screen — without installing three things or opening ten tabs. It's called **OpenlibExtended**, it's open source, and this guide is its listing: what it is, what its 1.6.0 brings, which permissions it asks for and by what name, and where it fits in the reader family we've already published.

## 📖 The listing

**OpenlibExtended** — an open-source client for Anna's Archive, with a built-in EPUB and PDF reader.

https://github.com/warreth/OpenlibExtended

- **License:** AGPL-3.0 — the code can be read, and it gets read.
- **Version:** 1.6.0 (September 2026) · 32.6 MB · Android 7 and up.
- **Cross-platform:** Android, iOS, Windows, Linux and macOS — the library follows you from the house to the pocket.
- **Languages:** Spanish, English, French, German and Russian, among others.
- **The core idea:** it searches **LibGen, Z-Library and Anna's Archive at the same time**, with automatic fallback if one source fails — and every result and every download shows where it came from. The source gets declared, as in this trade.

The built-in reader (EPUB and PDF) has configurable tap zones and gestures; the library manages your collection, shows trends and exports files to other apps if you'd rather read elsewhere.

## ✨ What 1.6.0 brings

- **Much faster downloads:** no polling of mirrors — fast hosts get priority.
- **Triple search** with automatic fallback across the three sources.
- **The visible source** on every result and every download.
- **Fixed covers** (the missing ones now show).
- **A what's-new screen** after updating, with one-tap mirror migration.
- **Custom library sections** and synced reading positions.
- **Smaller Android builds** and Scoop support on desktop.
- **Beta TTS:** the book read aloud, in testing phase.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is it official from Anna's Archive?**
No: it's an open-source client for it. The app's notice declares it with no detours.

**Why is it faster than I remembered?**
1.6.0 stopped polling mirrors one by one: it prioritizes the fast hosts directly. And if you're coming from an old version, mirror migration is one tap on the what's-new screen.

**Does it work without internet?**
What's downloaded, yes: the library and reader work locally. Triple search needs the network.

**And the TTS?**
Beta — it gets tested, noted, awaited. Like everything in this house.
