## ⚡ The trench, on one page

This house's owner said to dive into the sea's depths, in parallel — that not all life is found in space — and brought Facundo Cabral's key: *"porque uno no vive solo… por eso si hay uno, hay dos"* (because one doesn't live alone… that's why if there's one, there's two). This guide is the dive: **the bottom's eight houses** (the second basement, where the comic servers live and the readers nobody watches because they don't have ten thousand stars), **the day's dead one's register**, **the twins that showed up when touching each house** — because below, as above, nobody exists alone — and **the deep current**: the forty repos feeding all the house's apps, forty of forty alive, twenty-six pushing code TODAY itself. Dive of September 15, with the official server's stick and releases' atoms.

## ❓ Trench questions

**Why dive down if there are stars up top?**
Because the bottom holds up the sea: stump and Komga serve what the apps above read; the extensions' current feeds even the king. And because below, the truth shows without the shine: here a still house gets told apart from a living one.

**A manga translator, twice?**
Twice — and neither is spare: koharu for the study with local detection and inpainting models; Frank Yomik for editing the translation over the original art. The trade, like everything in the sea, has layers.
