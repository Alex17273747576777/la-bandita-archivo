## ⚡ The nursery, on one page

This guide recommends nothing for daily use — and its name says so: it's the **nursery**. Here live the sprouts: young, small, alpha projects — the ones this house's owner ordered watched while the ripe fruit gets harvested (Mihon, KOReader, Cloudstream and company, already on their shelves). Thirteen nursery listings verified TODAY, September 15, with the official server's and the REST's stick — and a double joy: two succession chains this house had told by deep expedition are now **certified by git itself**. The nursery's rule, in one line: if a sprout has real updated releases and passes the treaty's criteria, it enters the nursery — maybe — **under observation for the future**, never as a daily recommendation.

## 📜 The ripe fruit's rule

The owner's saying, recorded in the house's Map (rule 9): the doors get eaten by the ripe fruit; the sprouts get watched. A sprout with a real, dated release enters the nursery with a listing; a sprout without releases (only code pushing) gets noted as a leaf — not as an app. And neither leaves the nursery until time makes them trees. Daily use gets recommended from the shelves; the future gets watched from here.

## ❓ Nursery questions

**Why separate sprouts from recommendations?**
Because the reader deserves to know which shelf they're looking at: the ripe fruit gets recommended for today; the sprout gets watched for tomorrow. Mixing them is gifting illusions — or hiding futures.

**When does a sprout ascend?**
When time and the treaty say so: sustained real releases, an open house, and the use one has given it oneself. The nursery doesn't run: it repots when the plant asks.
