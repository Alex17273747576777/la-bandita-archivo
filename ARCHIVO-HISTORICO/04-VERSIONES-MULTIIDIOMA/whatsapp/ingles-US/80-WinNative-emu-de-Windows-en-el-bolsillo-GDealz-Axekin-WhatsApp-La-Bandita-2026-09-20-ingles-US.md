## 🪟 WinNative — the community-built Windows x86_64 emu

https://github.com/WinNative-Emu/WinNative

```
★591 · GPL-3.0
v0.6.2-beta (September 12, 2026)
What it is: «An Android app for playing Windows games
from Steam, Epic Games, GOG, and more on your
device» — its description; and its README (read
TODAY) makes it precise: a high-performance Windows
(x86_64) emulation environment unifying the best of
Winlator Bionic and Pluvia
```

What its house declares, with dates:

- **Connected libraries:** Steam, Epic and GOG — games are added by hand or the library syncs. Your account, your games, business as usual.
- **Retro consoles alongside:** from NES to PlayStation 2, with its own documentation (docs/RETRO-CONSOLES.md) — the Windows emu and the retro cabin under the same roof.
- **Frame generation:** LSFG and DIS, documented in its README (docs/FRAME-GENERATION.md) — the frame stuffing that used to be a thing for expensive PCs.
- **Installation:** the APK comes from its Releases; on first launch it lets the ImageFS install and you play. Four variants, the same app with a different package name: **Vanilla** (standard, to coexist with other forks), **Ludashi** (forces GPU and CPU to max clocks on some devices), **Antutu** (forces GPU clocks on most — benchmark spoof, its own README says so) and **Pubg** (a PUBG package name that unlocks some Game Booster features).

The Antutu and Ludashi variants deserve the house lesson: in Piece 10 we told why a sourceless benchmark doesn't get cited even by mistake. Here the repo **declares openly** what its variants do — and that's the right way: whoever measures should know what they're measuring. The card is noted with that date and that honesty.

Its own community on Discord, GPL-3.0 license, and a beta that calls itself beta: v0.6.2-beta. You test it, you log it, you wait — like the TTS in Piece 18. A young project with craft: its document tree (BUILDING, CREDITS, EMULATOR_CREDITS) betrays a tidy workshop.

The emu's natural companion: if WinNative runs the library, GDealz watches the price of what goes into it. Ad-free — which at this gate is already a statement of principles.

## ❓ Frequently asked questions

**Is WinNative official from Valve, Epic or GOG?**
No: it's a community-built environment (community-built, its README says) that connects your libraries — the way a reader connects your sources. Your account, your games, your terms.

**Why «beta» with 591 stars?**
Stars measure community, not maturity. Its own release says v0.6.2-beta — and this house trusts the repo's word over the number's enthusiasm.

**Do the Ludashi and Antutu variants cheat at benchmarks?**
They force clocks to the max, and their README declares it point-blank. The house doesn't prescribe benchmarks — but it does note when a project tells the factory truth. The Piece 10 lesson, in reverse: that's how it's done.

**Does GDealz give the games away?**
No: it flags the giveaways other stores hand out. The giveaway comes from the store; the app is the hound dog that keeps you from missing it.

**And why won't Axekin open?**
It opens — for people, not for machines. Its anti-bot wall is legitimate and respected: test the store in your browser, the day you need it.
