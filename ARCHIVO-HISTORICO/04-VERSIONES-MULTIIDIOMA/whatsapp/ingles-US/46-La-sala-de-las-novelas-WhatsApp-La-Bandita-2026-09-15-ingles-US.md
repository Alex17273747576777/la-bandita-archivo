## ⚡ The room, on one page

Reading's fourth door is the quietest: **the novels' room** — light novels, webnovels, long stories read by chapters. Six listings from the Miyomi catalog, all six with a verified house TODAY, September 15, and a find that justifies the stick of going down into the descriptions: this room's silent giant has been speaking **Chinese** for years — and the house that catalogued it never translated it. With this door, the room completes the reader's collection: pocket (36), desktop (40), comics in the trench (42), and now novels.

## ❓ Room questions

**Six apps to read novels?**
Six different trades: the multi-source giant (with its own language), the extensible, the one reading aloud to you, the translated one, the EPUB hauler and the all-in-one. The room doesn't repeat: it distributes.

**And the giant's Chinese?**
