## ⚡ The ambiguous ones, on one page

In this house's inventory there were eleven names without a last name: loose words the repository search engine didn't know how to measure. The owner's lesson explained it before this batch existed: you can't measure a fish with the tree-climbing stick — and each name got THE ITS OWN looked for: the web for some, the F-Droid catalog for others, the curated list for the third, the fork declaring its mother for the fourth. The result: **seven identified and verified** TODAY, September 15, two successions hiding inside the names — and four that honestly still lack a clear house. No tree to the fish; no fish to the tree.

## 🔍 The identified (seven)

- **NFile** — ★404 · v1.0.44 (Jul 12) · https://github.com/Senzme/NFile — the pretty file manager made in Flutter: "100% free, open-source, and completely ad-free," its author said when presenting it. Stick: the FOSS community that showed it first.
- **SelfLock** — ★2 · v1.0.0 (Jul 18) · https://github.com/EtashTyagi/SelfLock — the digital-wellbeing lock: schedules and app lists, the rest blocked. Newborn — and therefore listed with both sources: the house and the fork declaring it. Stars aren't the stick of birth.
- **Language Selector** — ★769 · 1.04 (May 2023) · https://github.com/VegaBobo/Language-Selector — per-app language via Shizuku (Android 13+): the original. Its name matched the house letter by letter — the simplest stick: exactness.
- **WidgetsPro** — ★168 · 1.2.7 (Jan 20) · https://github.com/preethamkmr3/WidgetsPro — "Widgets inspired from Nothing OS design language," with a CPU monitor via Shizuku. Stick: its own store listing, which cites its repository.
- **Smart Edge** — ★362 · v1.3.6 (Jun 13) · https://github.com/Imtiaz-Official/Smart-Edge — the OriginOS-style sidebar: apps, screenshots and windows at one swipe. On F-Droid. Stick: the exact name, hyphen included.
- **DeskControl** — ★61 · 1.3.13 (Aug 8) · https://github.com/exiarepairii/deskcontrol — turns the phone into touchpad and mouse for an app running on a wired external screen. Stick: the curated Shizuku list, describing it word for word the same.
- **ADNS** — ★628 · v2.1.5 (Sep 12) · https://github.com/eyalm2000/adns — "Android's Private DNS Supercharged": the system's private DNS with a native NextDNS panel, reproducible builds and declared FOSS flavor. Stick: the NextDNS community pointing to it as the native one.

## 🧬 Two successions hidden in the names

The ambiguous ones kept genealogy nobody had told:

1. **Language Selector → Language Selector Neo.** The original (VegaBobo) has been still since 2023; its fork **Neo** (ezn24, ★10, v1.1.1 of Aug 22) took the wheel with dependency maintenance and new support. The UAD→UAD-NG pattern, again — now inside a name that looked like one.
2. **smart-edge → Smart Edge.** abh80's "alternative to the dynamic island" (★234) carries its last release from 2022; Imtiaz's sidebar took the name — with a hyphen — and the pulse (v1.3.6, on F-Droid). Two different apps that shared a word: the living one signs at the door with its hyphen.

## 🌫️ The ones still without a clear house

Honest radar — names that even with their stick gave no unambiguous owner: **Hamburger** (the search only finds menus inside other apps), **MagicDesk** (the name belongs to Commodore 64 cartridges — the Android one, if it exists, doesn't declare itself), **Sentry File Manager** (error SDKs dispute the name), and **PI** (two letters, too many candidates, none sure). What can't be sustained doesn't get listed: they stay on the radar with their why, and whatever link shows up will close each case.

## 🚪 The Android door, closed and squared

With this batch, the inventory's **Android, system, root and Shizuku** door stands **complete**: 173 candidates processed — sixty-some listings verified across seven publications (28–34), the catalogs sent to their trade as sources, the other doors' names delegated to their batches, the archived ones to the Register, the radars with a date and a why. The door that started as "197 listings without a link" ends with a house, a date and a memory. The wall's other doors await their turn — each with its fish and its trees.

## ❓ Ambiguous-ones questions

**Why did these names cost more than the others?**
Because the stick wasn't theirs: the search engine measures popular repositories, and these names were small, new or disputed. Stick changed — catalog, web, curated list, fork declaring its mother — they got measured in one pass.

**And if a radar turns out to be nothing?**
That's a result too: the radar says "name without a house as of this date." If the link shows up someday, it closes the case; if it doesn't, honesty already did its job.
