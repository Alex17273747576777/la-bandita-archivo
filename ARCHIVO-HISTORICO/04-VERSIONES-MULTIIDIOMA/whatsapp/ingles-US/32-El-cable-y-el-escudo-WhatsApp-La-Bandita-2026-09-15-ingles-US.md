## ❓ Cable-and-shield questions

**Do I need the PC for all this?**
For flashing and ADB, yes — that's why it's called the cable. For the shield, no: NetGuard, PCAPdroid and TrackerControl live entirely on the phone.

**Can an updater update modules too?**
The one that already exists: UpgradeAll declares it at its door. Same as always — first you read what the house says about itself, then you test slowly.
