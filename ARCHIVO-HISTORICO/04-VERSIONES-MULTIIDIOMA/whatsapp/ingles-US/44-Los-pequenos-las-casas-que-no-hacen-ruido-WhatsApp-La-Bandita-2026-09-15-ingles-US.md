## ❓ Little-ones questions

**Is a twelve-star app worth it?**
It depends on the gap: if the couch's TV has no tracker, NyanTV is worth more than any giant. The star measures applause, not utility — and in this house dates rule, not applause.

**Why do so many do the same thing?**
The basin again: each family builds its own for its language, its screen, its design. Whoever wants one single app already has the big guides; whoever wants their exact gap, is here.
