## ⚡ The living room, on one page

The wall's anime-and-video door kept 162 listings — and opening them TODAY turned up a context the wall never explained: **this is not another branch of anime clients** (those already have their guides: Piece 11 AniZen, 13 Aniyomi/Animetail/Tadami, 14 Animiru/Anikku). The wall's living room is another thing: **IPTV for the television, Jellyfin clients, and players asking for a big screen**. Seven alive verified at source today, September 15 — two of them moved code this morning — and one passing to the Register.

## ❓ Two living-room questions

**Why does this guide barely talk about anime?**
Because the house already has three anime-client guides (11, 13, 14). This wall's door turned out to be the IPTV, Jellyfin and players room — and telling it as "anime" would have repeated the wall's mistake: categories without looking inside.

**Which IPTV do I use then?**
That question isn't this house's to answer: the player is open source and goes listed; the channel list and its keys are your business and your provider's. Here everybody connects their own.
