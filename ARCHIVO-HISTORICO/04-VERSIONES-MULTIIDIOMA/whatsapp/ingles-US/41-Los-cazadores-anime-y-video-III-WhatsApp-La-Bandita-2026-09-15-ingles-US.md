## ❓ Hunters questions

**Fourteen apps for the same thing?**
Not for the same: each hunter hunts in its own forest — tracking, streaming, torrents, desktop, TV, the apple. This guide doesn't order installing fourteen: it shows the forest's map and lets each reader pick their hunter.

**Why dates and not stars?**
Because stars decorate and dates rule: NineAnimator has over eight hundred and five years of silence; animestream has four hundred and shipped a version yesterday. The nursery and the shelf get separated by time, not by applause.
