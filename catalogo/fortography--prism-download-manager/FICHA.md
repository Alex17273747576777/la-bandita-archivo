*The fast, beautiful, open-source download manager for Windows, macOS and Linux — up to 32 connections, browser integration, video & MP3 downloads, crash-safe resume.*

## Qué es, según quien lo hace

> Prism splits every download into up to 32 parallel connections, keeps them all busy until the last byte, and survives crashes, sleep and pulled cables. Click a link in your browser and Prism takes over; hover a video and grab it as MP4 in the quality you want — or just the MP3. All of it in a calm, liquid-glass interface with dark and light themes.
