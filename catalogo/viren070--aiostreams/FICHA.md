# AIOStreams · Viren070

*AIOStreams brings your Stremio addons, debrid services and Usenet together into one list, filtered, sorted and formatted your way, and plays it in Stremio or in its own apps for every screen.*

**Racimo:** reemplazo-del-stack · **Repositorio:** `Viren070/AIOStreams` · **Estado:** vivo · **Medido:** 2026-11-05 11:42 UTC

## Qué es, según quien lo hace

> Instead of juggling a dozen addons, each with its own settings and its own way of listing results, you configure AIOStreams once. It asks every source you've added, removes the duplicates, filters, sorts and names what comes back by your rules, and hands you one clean list, wherever you watch.
