# Square · Lelonio

*Unofficial YouTube Music / Spotify Premium client for Android, built in Liquid Glass. Audio effects, Spotify Connect, listening history, 8 languages. Rust core on librespot.*

**Racimo:** clientes-de-musica · **Repositorio:** `Lelonio/Square` · **Estado:** vivo · **Medido:** 2026-11-05 11:44 UTC

## Qué es, según quien lo hace

> - Spotify or YouTube Music, chosen at first launch and switchable in the settings. Home, search, the library and the player work the same on both. - Spotify: your own account, with its playlists, library and Connect devices. Needs Premium. - YouTube Music: the whole catalogue and the music videos, with no account needed. Sign in with Google to bring in your playlists, albums and followed artists.
