## Qué es, según quien lo hace

> Local reading of content. A configurable reader with multiple viewers, reading directions and other settings. Tracker support: MangaBaka, MyAnimeList, AniList, Kitsu, MangaUpdates, Shikimori, Bangumi, and Hikka support. Categories to organize your library. Light and dark themes. Schedule updating your library for new chapters. Create backups locally to read offline or to your desired cloud service
