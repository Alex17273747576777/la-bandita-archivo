## Qué es, según quien lo hace

> --- OwnTV is a native Android TV IPTV player, built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for TV. It runs two playback engines — libmpv (FFmpeg) for the widest compatibility, ExoPlayer (Media3) for near-instant Live TV — and it is a player only: you bring your own Xtream login, M3U playlist (URL or a local file) or Stalker/Ministra portal.
