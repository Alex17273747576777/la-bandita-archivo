# MegaNexus · MegaNexusMediaPlayer

## Qué es, según quien lo hace

> MegaNexus replaces everything you see in Kodi — Home, menus, title pages, the player and the screensaver — with one app made for the TV remote. Like Stremio and Nuvio, it is driven by add-ons you add with a link: metadata add-ons bring the movies and series, stream add-ons bring the links to play, subtitle add-ons bring the subtitles.
